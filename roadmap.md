# Roadmap

## What this is
A study task list for people learning a language. Each task is tagged with a skill (Listening, Speaking, Reading or Writing) so learners can see which skill they have been avoiding.

## What Done means
A stranger opens the live site and creates an account with an email and password. They add a few study tasks, each tagged with one skill (Listening, Speaking, Reading or Writing), and mark some of them done. They see how many tasks they have finished for each skill, with the lowest one marked "Avoiding". They log out, come back later, log in, and find all their tasks and counts as they left them. That is the whole project. Anything more goes in the Backlog.

## Slices
1. Sign up and log in | done-criteria: (1) On the live site while logged out, click Sign up and enter a new fake email and a password; you land on a page that shows "Signed in as" and that email. (2) Click Log out; you are back on the logged-out page, and typing the site address plus /tasks into the address bar sends you to the login page. (3) Log in with that email and a wrong password; an error message appears and you are still logged out. (4) Log in with the right password, close the tab, open the site again; it still shows "Signed in as" and that email. | status: ACTIVE
2. Add and complete tagged tasks | done-criteria: (1) While signed in, type "Listen to one podcast episode", choose Listening, click Add; the task appears in the list labeled "Listening". (2) Refresh, log out, log back in; the task is still there, still labeled Listening. (3) Click Done on the task and refresh; it still shows as done. (4) Log in with a second fake account; none of the first account's tasks appear. | status: pending
3. See which skill you are avoiding | done-criteria: (1) Sign in with an account that has finished 2 Listening tasks, 1 Reading task, and no Speaking or Writing tasks; the summary shows Listening 2, Reading 1, Speaking 0, Writing 0. (2) On that account, Speaking and Writing both carry the label "Avoiding", and Listening and Reading do not. (3) Add a Speaking task, mark it done, refresh; Speaking shows 1 and only Writing says "Avoiding". (4) Sign in with a brand-new account; the summary shows all four skills at 0 and the message "No tasks done yet", not a blank area or an error. | status: pending
